Delivery Risk for Jira

Privacy policy

What the app reads, writes and stores. Which is very little.

Delivery Risk for Jira Last updated: 7 September 2026

The short version

This app runs entirely inside Atlassian's infrastructure. It has no servers, no database of its own, and no analytics. Nothing it reads about your Jira leaves Atlassian.

What the app reads

When you run a query, the app reads the following fields from the Jira issues your query returns, using your own Jira permissions:

  • Issue key, summary, issue type and status
  • Project key
  • Start date and due date
  • Assignee display name
  • Parent issue key
  • Issue links of the "blocks" and "depends on" kind

It reads nothing else. It does not read comments, attachments, worklogs, descriptions, or any issue your own Jira permissions would not already show you.

What the app writes

Two fields, on issues you explicitly choose to reschedule, and only when you press Publish:

  • Due date
  • The start-date field your Jira uses, when it has one

It never creates, deletes, transitions, assigns or comments on an issue. There is no delete path in the application at all.

What the app stores

Unpublished changes are held in Atlassian's Forge storage, scoped to your own Atlassian account, so that a draft survives a page reload. Each record is an issue key with two dates. Pressing Publish or Discard removes it.

Nothing else is stored. No profile, no history, no usage record.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. The app declares no external egress permissions and makes no requests to any host outside Atlassian. It is built on Atlassian Forge and qualifies for the Runs on Atlassian programme, which means Atlassian hosts the compute and the storage.

The AI feature

The optional Explain button sends a summary of the conflicts already shown on screen to a large language model. That model is hosted by Atlassian, runs inside Atlassian's infrastructure, and the request does not leave it. No third-party AI service is used, and no data is sent to the app author.

What is sent: issue keys, project keys, dates, statuses and the first 60 characters of issue summaries, for at-risk issues only, capped at 60 issues. The answer is cached in Forge storage against a hash of that summary so the same plan is not sent twice.

The feature runs only when you press the button. It never runs automatically.

Cookies and tracking

None. The app sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and contains no third-party scripts or fonts. Its fonts are bundled with the app for this reason.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage, including any unpublished drafts. Dates already published to Jira are ordinary Jira data and remain under your control.

Your rights

The app processes your Jira data solely to display it back to you. The controller of that data is your own organisation, through its Atlassian subscription. Requests about data held in Jira should go to your Jira administrator and to Atlassian.
