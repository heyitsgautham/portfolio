Support

How to get help, what to include, and how fast you will hear back.

Contact

Email support@gauthamkrishna.dev. Gautham Krishna, who built the app, reads and answers every message.

Many questions are already answered in the documentation, including what each part of the board means and why the app sometimes says its answer is incomplete.

What to include

  • Your Jira site, for example yourcompany.atlassian.net.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The exact wording of any message the app showed, or a screenshot of the board.
  • The JQL query in the box at the top, if the board looks wrong.

Never send a password or an API token. The app does not need one, and we will never ask for one.

Response times

Business days are Monday to Friday, India Standard Time.

RequestFirst reply
Critical: the app stops working for everyone on your site, or writes a wrong date to JiraWithin 24 hours
Security vulnerabilityWithin 2 business days, as set out in the security policy
Everything elseWithin 5 business days

What support covers

  • Questions about using the app.
  • Bugs, including wrong conflicts, wrong dates, and errors on the board.
  • Feature requests, which are read and answered but not promised.

The app is free. Fixes reach every site automatically, so you never need to update it yourself.
